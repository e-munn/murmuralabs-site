# Video Script — Intro to murmur

**Length target:** ~2 minutes (≈300 words at a calm pace)
**Format:** Screen recording with voiceover (or talking-head intro → screen share)
**Tone:** Confident, grounded, not salesy. Show the tool — let it speak.

---

## Script

**[0:00 — On camera, brief]**

Hi — I'm [YOUR NAME], founder of Murmura Labs.

My background is in urban sciences and software development.

I wanted to send a quick video to introduce a tool we've built. It's easier to show than to describe, so let me get straight to it.

**[0:20 — Switch to screen share: murmur full city map]**

This is **murmur**. It's a city intelligence platform.

What you're looking at is an entire city, modeled at the neighborhood level. Every hexagon is a zone — thousands of them — and each one carries 118 real-world data fields: demographics, health outcomes, pollution burden, housing pressure, transit access, school proximity, economic indicators.

All of it from public sources — Census, CalEnviroScreen, CDC PLACES, Zillow, GTFS transit feeds, EPA, OpenStreetMap. Nothing proprietary. Nothing black-box.

**[0:55 — Click into a hex cell]**

When I click on any zone, I see its full profile. Income distribution, rent burden, pollution exposure, health outcomes — all in one card.

**[1:10 — Click a contrasting cell]**

Click another neighborhood, and you see a completely different reality. Same city, two different worlds. That contrast is the starting point.

**[1:25 — Run a scenario]**

Now — here's what makes it different. When I apply a scenario — an investment, a rezoning, a new transit line, an infrastructure project — the platform computes the cascading impact across the *entire* city.

Not just the project site. Who benefits. Who's harmed. Where displacement risk concentrates. How equity outcomes shift, neighborhood by neighborhood.

**[1:50 — Pull back to the full map]**

That's the core idea: see the second-order effects of a decision before it's made.

**[2:00 — Back on camera, brief]**

If this is useful for the work you're doing, I'd welcome fifteen minutes to walk through it together — on your city's data.

Thank you.

— [YOUR NAME], Murmura Labs.

---

## Production notes

- **Pacing:** Aim for 145–155 words/min. If you run long, cut the data-source list — collapse to "all from public sources."
- **Open with eye contact**, not a logo. The first three seconds carry the credibility.
- **Screen share is the demo.** Mouse moves slowly. No fast clicks. Let the viewer track what you're showing.
- **One scenario, one moment of contrast.** Don't run multiple scenarios — overload kills the punch.
- **End frame:** Hold on yourself for one beat after "Thank you" before cutting.
- **CTA lives in the email, not the video.** Send with: "Two-minute video below — at the end I ask for fifteen minutes. Happy to come to you."
