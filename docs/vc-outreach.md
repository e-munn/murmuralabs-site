# murmur — VC outreach plan

Outreach targets, lanes, and the decision that shapes the whole list.

---

## The framing decision (resolve before sending anything)

Two viable positionings, each implying a different VC list:

**Positioning A — Vertical govtech / urban intelligence**
*"murmur is a city intelligence platform for planners and EJ orgs. Buyer is government. Moat is data integration + relationships."*

- Realistic outcome: $20–100M company over 7–10 years. Services-heavy revenue. Sticky contracts.
- Fundable by: vertical govtech / urban VCs, civic tech accelerators, philanthropic capital.
- This is what murmur looks like *today*.

**Positioning B — Platform / urban simulation substrate**
*"murmur is the simulation engine for urban systems. Buyer is developers + AI labs. Moat is model quality."*

- Realistic outcome: $500M–$1B company over 10+ years. SaaS / API economics. Higher risk.
- Fundable by: deep-tech VCs (Pebblebed, A16z infra, etc.), foundation models VCs.
- This is a different company. Don't reframe just to chase the bigger fund — only do this if you actually believe the platform play.

**Recommendation:** pitch Positioning A first. It matches what exists. If you want to *test* Positioning B, send Pebblebed a one-paragraph reframe and use their reaction as data. Don't commit to the reframe based on one fund's interest.

- Julie Lein, Clara Brenner — Managing Partners. Andrew Felbinger — Partner.
- Pre-seed / seed, $500K–$3.5M checks.
- Local — coffee, not just email.

- Site: urbaninnovationfund.com

- Slightly broader than UIF — includes mobility, energy, civic infra.

### Govtech Fund
- Ron Bouganim, SF. Narrow govtech-only thesis.
- Smaller fund, fewer checks, but every check is on-thesis.

### Bloomberg Beta

- Earlier stage, $500K–$2M typical. Operator-friendly.

### Equal Ventures
- Verticals: climate, insurance, government services.
- Rick Zullo. NY-based but invests nationally.

### 8VC (govtech vertical)
- Joe Lonsdale's fund. Has a real govtech book (Palantir alumni network).
- Larger checks, later stage typically — better as a follow-on than first money.

## Tier 2 — Civic tech accelerators / non-dilutive capital

Real money, no equity dilution, slower process. Worth running in parallel with Tier 1.

### Bloomberg Philanthropies — Mayors Challenge
- Funds city-level innovation pilots. Could fund Richmond directly to deploy murmur.
- Cycles run periodically — check the current cohort timing.

### Knight Foundation
- Civic tech grants. Strong in journalism + civic engagement overlap.
- knightfoundation.org/grants

### Fast Forward
- Tech-nonprofit accelerator. Murmur isn't a nonprofit but they sometimes work with hybrid civic-tech for-profits.

### NSF SBIR
- Small Business Innovation Research grants. Non-dilutive, ~$275K Phase I.
- Civic tech / smart cities is in scope. Real paperwork, real money, no equity.

### What Works Cities (Results for America)
- Not a funder directly, but a 200+ city network. Distribution > capital here.
- A WWC endorsement or pilot is a credibility multiplier for VC fundraising.

- Site: pebblebed.com

### Innovation Endeavors (Eric Schmidt's fund)

- Larger checks, later stage typically.
- "American Dynamism" framing is a real wedge for murmur.

## Sequencing — what to do this month

1. **Lock the positioning.** Vertical (A) is the default; only deviate if you've decided differently.
2. **Ship the 1min trailer.** Cheapest unlock; everything else assumes it exists.
3. **Send Tier 1 in one batch.** Don't drip — UIF, Urban.us, Govtech Fund, Bloomberg Beta, Equal Ventures. Same week. Different intros if possible.
4. **Apply for one grant in parallel.** NSF SBIR or Knight. Long lead time, start now.
5. **Pebblebed as an experiment** (one paragraph, one trailer, one email). Treat their response as positioning data, not a referendum.
6. **Two warm-intro requests** to adjacent operators (Replica or Tolemi alum). One conversation here reshapes your pitch faster than VC feedback does.

---

## What to *not* do

- Don't send to A16z, Sequoia, Benchmark cold. Top-tier generalist VCs are a waste of cycles at this stage unless you have a warm partner intro. Wait until UIF / Urban.us interest gives you signal to escalate.
- Don't send the 5–10min engineer video as a first touch. Wrong format for cold inbox.
- Don't pitch the platform reframe to vertical VCs (or vice versa). Confused positioning is worse than narrow positioning.
- Don't take VC money you don't need. If a Richmond pilot pays $50–150K, that's runway with no dilution. Run that path in parallel and only raise when the city contracts plateau.
